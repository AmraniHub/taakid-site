Privacy Policy

Taakid, by Amrania Labs · Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Contact: amrani4online@gmail.com

Taakid ("the App") is a Shopify application that helps merchants confirm cash-on-delivery orders with their buyers before shipping them. This policy explains what data the App handles, why, and what rights merchants and buyers have.

Our role

The merchant who installs the App is the data controller for their buyers' data. Amrania Labs processes that data on the merchant's behalf, only to provide the App. Shopify Inc. holds the order data as the merchant's commerce platform.

The App does not read or store buyer email addresses, street addresses, payment card data, or browsing behaviour. Messages are sent by the merchant from their own WhatsApp or phone; the App never sends messages itself and never sees their content after the merchant opens the chat.

The checkout rule

If the merchant turns it on, a Shopify Function decides at checkout whether to offer cash on delivery. It receives a list of short hashes of the phone numbers of buyers who refused a delivery, computed with a random per-store salt, and compares them with a hash of the phone number entered at checkout. The Function runs inside Shopify; no checkout data is sent to the App's servers.

Data about merchants

The store's domain, the App settings (message templates, languages, hold and checkout-rule options) and the subscription plan. Billing is handled entirely by Shopify.

Retention and deletion

  • Order statuses are kept while the App is installed so the merchant can see their history.
  • When Shopify sends a customers/redact request, the App deletes that buyer's phone hash, counters and order records.
  • When the merchant uninstalls, sessions and plan data are deleted at once, and Shopify's shop/redact request 48 hours later deletes everything else.

Security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database run by our hosting provider, Railway, on a private network reachable only by the App. Webhooks from Shopify are verified by HMAC signature. Connections use TLS.

Your rights

Buyers can ask the merchant to access or delete their data; the merchant's request reaches the App through Shopify and is acted on automatically. Merchants can contact us at amrani4online@gmail.com for any privacy question. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page if the App's data handling changes, and change the date above.
